H1  Encoder adaptation
five established methods and one input-side variant; all read the last layer with a gradient-trained head
LoRA
Trains: low-rank branch B·A in parallel to W₀ (q, v projections), plus the head.
DoRA
Trains: the same branch on the direction of W₀, plus a per-channel magnitude m, plus the head.
AdaLoRA
Trains: a low-rank branch in singular-value form whose rank budget moves across sites during training, plus the head.
IA³
Trains: a per-channel gate ℓ on the output of each projection (no rank), plus the head.
HiRA
Trains: a low-rank product multiplied element-wise by W₀, giving a high-rank update at LoRA's budget, plus the head.
Radial-LoRA — input-side variant
Trains: LoRA's branch on a norm-tempered input gλ(u)·u; λ is fixed by rank (0.35 at r=8), not trained. Same budget as LoRA.
H2  The readout
do not adapt; read the frozen encoder in closed form (proposed)
DFA
Trains: nothing. Equal-weight (1/K) fusion of all K = L+1 depths, standardisation, Ledoit–Wolf LDA fitted once.
DFA-last — depth control
Trains: nothing. Same head on the last layer only; isolates what depth fusion adds.
Controls
probe depth of the read, estimator and encoder adaptation
LoRA, all-layer read — lora-ln-dw
Trains: as LoRA, but the head reads the fixed uniform average of all layers. lora-recipe adds test-time checkpoint averaging.
Learned layer weights — wls-readout
Trains: the layer weights and the head by gradient on the frozen encoder (SUPERB-style); stage 1.
